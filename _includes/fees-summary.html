Fees & Scheduling

My practice is private pay and structured around every-other-week appointments. Individual therapy is $200 for a 55-minute session, so most months of ongoing therapy cost $400.

Superbills (detailed receipts you can submit to your insurance company) are available on request for out-of-network benefits.
