# Fees & Scheduling

## Fees and payment

I am a **private-pay therapist**.

- **Individual therapy:** $200 for 55 minutes
- **Initial intake:** $285
- **[Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART)](/blog/accelerated-resolution-therapy/):** $300 for up to 80 minutes
- **[Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy (KAP)](/kap/):** separate pricing, described below

Because my practice is structured around every-other-week appointments, most months of ongoing individual therapy cost $400.

A credit card on file is required before scheduling your first appointment. Payment is charged at the time of each session.

I do not bill insurance directly. If you have out-of-network benefits, I can provide a **superbill upon request** that you may submit to your insurance company for possible reimbursement.

### A note about diagnosis and insurance

Insurance reimbursement requires a mental health diagnosis and medical necessity. Some people are comfortable with this; others have understandable concerns about having a documented mental health diagnosis or sharing it with an insurance company.

If you choose to use out-of-network benefits, we'll discuss diagnosis openly and make sure you understand what information will be shared.

### Ketamine-Assisted Psychotherapy

KAP involves costs beyond my therapy fees. The medical evaluation and prescription are handled separately through [Symmetry Counseling](https://symcounseling.com/ketamine-services/) and their medical prescriber, and you'll purchase the medication at your pharmacy. We'll discuss the total cost, including preparation, the medicine session, and integration, before you decide whether to proceed.

### Good Faith Estimates

If you're not using insurance, you're entitled to a Good Faith Estimate of your treatment costs. If your bill exceeds that estimate by $400 or more, you may have the right to dispute it. Learn more at [cms.gov/nosurprises](https://cms.gov/nosurprises/).

## Every-other-week sessions

My practice is structured around **every-other-week sessions**, at a standing appointment time. For many people, this makes ongoing therapy a more manageable commitment of both time and money. It also gives you some time to see how things we discuss in therapy play out in your actual life.

Some of my favorite sessions begin with a client saying, "Okay, I have a list."

If you need **weekly support during a difficult stretch, telehealth sessions are available on the alternate weeks.**

My practice is primarily in person at my Murray office. Limited telehealth appointments may be available by arrangement for clients physically located in Utah. Telehealth can also be used occasionally when weather, illness, or other circumstances make meeting in person impractical.

## How long should I be in therapy?

Sometimes therapy is targeted and brief. You may need some psychoeducation, a few focused sessions, or [Accelerated Resolution Therapy (ART)](/blog/accelerated-resolution-therapy/) to address a particular experience that continues to distress you.

Other work takes longer. Changing longstanding patterns in how you relate to yourself and other people can take considerable time. A year and a half or two years of consistent therapy is not unusual.

I don't assume therapy should be brief, and I don't assume it should continue indefinitely. My goal is to **work myself out of a job.** If long-term therapy is meaningful and useful to you, I'm comfortable with that, but I don't want us to keep rehashing the same material just because therapy has become the default. If our work isn't helping, let's talk about that and consider whether a different approach or another clinician might serve you better.

## Practice policies

Because your appointment time is reserved for you, I ask for **48 hours' notice** if you need to cancel or reschedule. Late cancellations are charged **$100**, and missed appointments (no-shows) are charged **$150**.

Once you're a client, the secure client portal is the best way to send me messages about your therapy. Email and text are best reserved for scheduling and other logistics. I generally respond within one business day, but I don't provide emergency or on-demand clinical support between appointments.

## What to expect

### Your first session

In our first appointment, we'll talk about what brings you in, what you'd like to be different, relevant history, and what you've already tried. My goal is for you to leave feeling understood, with some hope that things can change and a sense of where we might begin.

I'll usually share some initial impressions and ideas about how we might approach the work. Sometimes there's a fairly clear direction, such as a specific issue that may respond well to [ART](/blog/accelerated-resolution-therapy/). Other times we'll need more time to figure that out.

The intake is also a chance for both of us to see how the fit feels. If I think another clinician would be a better fit, I'll tell you and offer referrals when I can. And if *you* decide I'm not the right therapist for you, I genuinely appreciate knowing that.

### Subsequent sessions

In NARM, I often begin by asking, **"What would you hope to get out of our time together today?"** Some people love that question. Some people find it surprisingly stressful. "I don't know" is a perfectly good place to start.

We don't need to have all your goals figured out at the beginning of therapy. Often, what you want becomes clearer as we work together.

### What to bring

You don't need to bring anything specific. If you have thoughts, questions, or topics you want to make sure we discuss, you're welcome to bring notes. Feel free to bring a journal, water bottle, fidgets, or anything else that helps you feel comfortable.

### Privacy

If we happen to see each other outside the office, I won't approach you or acknowledge our professional relationship unless you initiate contact.

## Getting started

You can complete the entire process online. No phone calls required.

1. **Complete a brief online questionnaire.** Tell me a little about what brings you to therapy and what you're looking for.
2. **I'll review your inquiry.** You can generally expect a response within one business day. If it sounds like we may work well together, I'll invite you to my secure client portal.
3. **Complete your paperwork and schedule your intake.** Once you've completed the required forms, you can request an available intake appointment through the portal.

I don't offer introductory consultation calls.
