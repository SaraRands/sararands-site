# Website Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 24, 2026*

Your privacy matters to me. I collect a limited amount of information about how people use this website so I can understand what's useful and how people find me. I don't use advertising trackers or collect information to follow people's activity across different websites.

## Website Analytics

I use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics tool, to see how many people visit my website, where they come from, and which pages they read. I also look at general patterns of blog engagement, like how long articles are viewed and how far readers scroll. I'm interested in whether my writing is reaching people, not in tracking individual visitors. My analytics don't use tracking cookies or persistent visitor identifiers, and I don't receive information that identifies individual visitors. My analytics respect Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control browser settings.

I also use Google Search Console to understand how my website shows up in Google searches.

## Reaching Out About Therapy

If you're interested in working with me, I'll ask you to fill out a short Google Form with your contact information and a little about what brings you to therapy. Your responses are handled through my Google Workspace account, which has a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement in place. I use what you share to consider whether we'd be a good fit and to respond to your inquiry. I don't use this information for advertising or website analytics.

If we begin working together, I'll move the relevant information into my electronic health record system, where it's kept according to my clinical records policy. If we don't, I generally delete your inquiry information within 30 days after the inquiry is closed, unless I need to keep it for legal or professional reasons.

## Newsletter

If you subscribe to my newsletter, Buttondown handles your email address and sends the newsletters. I've turned off email open and click tracking. Links in my newsletters may include information that helps me see which articles bring people to my website. You can unsubscribe anytime using the link at the bottom of any newsletter.

## Videos, Podcasts, and Other Websites

Some pages include YouTube videos and Spotify podcast players. Those services may collect information about visitors under their own privacy policies. If you follow a link to another website, such as Google Maps, that website has its own privacy practices.

## Questions?

If you have questions about how this website handles your information, you're welcome to [contact me](/contact/).

This policy covers my public website. Information related to therapy services is also covered by my separate clinical privacy and informed consent documents.
